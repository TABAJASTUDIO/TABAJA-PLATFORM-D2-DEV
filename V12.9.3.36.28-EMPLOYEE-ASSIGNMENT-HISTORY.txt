V12.9.3.36.28 — Employee Assignment History

- Employee Center remains the master employee record.
- Existing active assignment is never overwritten when Area/Site/Post changes.
- Transfer requires an explicit Transfer Effective Date.
- Old assignment is ended the day before the transfer date.
- New assignment is inserted as ACTIVE with the same employee UUID.
- Assignment History is shown read-only in Employee Center.
- No Identity Designer, Payroll calculation, navigation, permissions, or Smart Identity logic changed.
