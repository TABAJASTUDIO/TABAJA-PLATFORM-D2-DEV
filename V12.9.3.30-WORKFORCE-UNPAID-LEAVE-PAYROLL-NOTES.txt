TABAJA SOLUTION DEV — V12.9.3.30
WORKFORCE UNPAID LEAVE PAYROLL ENGINE

Scope:
- DEV only.
- Smart Identity frozen; no Identity files changed.
- Monthly payroll: approved unpaid leave creates a real payroll deduction.
- Formula: contractual monthly salary / calendar days in payroll period * approved unpaid units.
- Paid leave creates no deduction.
- Daily/hourly/per-shift bases do not receive a second unpaid-leave deduction.
- Frozen payroll snapshot records policy, units, daily rate, amount and approved leave request details.
- Payroll Results UI displays the unpaid-leave deduction evidence under Deductions.
- Existing permission/scope guard remains in wf_calculate_payroll.
